Celebrations

Full-screen celebration shown after completing all lessons in a unit. Golden background for mastered units, green for standard completion. Shows unit title, lesson count, accuracy %, total XP earned, mascot pose (excited/laughing), confetti or stars effects, and share button. Streak Milestone Celebration

StreakMilestonemodal
Celebration modal for streak milestones at 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100+ days. Golden background with confetti effects. Shows milestone badge icon, streak flame with day count, gem rewards, unlock info (titles/profile frames), next milestone hint, and share button. Component: src/components/engagement/StreakMilestone.tsx.

Streak Continued Modal

StreakContinuedmodal
Displayed when user completes a lesson and continues their streak. Orange background with confetti. Component: src/components/engagement/StreakContinued.tsx.

League Winner Modal

LeagueWinnermodal
Shown when user finishes 1st in their weekly league. Golden background with fireworks effects. Shows champion mascot pose, "1st Place!" headline, league tier name, and continue button. Triggers once per week if user wins. Component: src/components/engagement/LeagueWinner.tsx.
Streaks

Streak Freeze Banner

StreakFreezebanner
Blue gradient banner shown after user activates a streak freeze. Shows snowflake icon and message "Your streak freeze saved your streak yesterday!" with dismissible X button. Component: src/components/engagement/StreakFreeze.tsx.

Streak Repair Modal

StreakFreezemodal
Purple background modal shown when user's streak breaks. Allows repair using gems (cost: 75 gems). Shows worried mascot, previous streak value, gem cost indicator, and "Skip" button. Only appears if there was an actual streak to repair. Component: src/components/engagement/StreakFreeze.tsx.

TrialPromptModalmodal

Banners

Active XP Event Banner

ActiveEventBannerbanner
Floating pill-shaped banner below course header showing active XP multiplier events (Power Hour, Weekend Double XP, League Sprint). Displays event icon, time remaining, multiplier badge (e.g., "2x"), and dismissible X button. Updates every 30 seconds. Positioned absolutely with fixed z-index. Component: src/components/ui/ActiveEventBanner.tsx.

Daily Limit Banner

DailyLimitBannerbanner
Amber banner shown to free users near their daily practice limit. Shows "Daily practice limit" label, remaining questions counter, progress bar, and upgrade link. Appears at the top of the lesson screen when approaching or at the limit. Only visible to free users (pro users have unlimited). Component: src/components/ui/DailyLimitBanner.tsx.

Email Verification Banner

EmailVerificationBannerbanner
Amber banner at page top reminding unverified users to verify email. Shows verification status message and "Resend" button (becomes "Verification email sent!" after sending). Dismissible with X button. Only shown once per session if verification is pending. Component: src/components/ui/EmailVerificationBanner.tsx.

Offline Banner

OfflineBannerbanner
Fixed position banner at bottom-left (or center on mobile) showing offline status. Shows pulsing red dot indicator and message "You're offline. Changes will sync when you reconnect." Auto-hides when connection restored. Dark background with white text. Component: src/components/ui/OfflineBanner.tsx.

Out of Hearts Modal

OutOfHeartsModalmodal
Red (#CE3030) full-screen modal shown when user runs out of practice hearts. Shows sad mascot, "Out of Hearts" headline, countdown timer to next heart, options to watch ad for free heart (non-pro only), buy individual heart (100 gems), or refill all (variable gem cost), and pro upgrade button. Toast & Notifications

Toast Notification

ToastNotificationtoast
Generic system toast for transient messages. White card with emoji icon, bold title, optional subtitle, rounded corners, and shadow. Auto-dismisses after 3.5s (configurable). Supports drag-to-dismiss and click-to-dismiss. Max 5 toasts queued, displays top 3. Used throughout app for notifications and alerts. Component: src/components/ui/ToastNotification.tsx (store + container + item).

Glossary Popover

GlossaryPopoverpopover
Floating popover (above or below anchor) showing glossary term definition. White card with rounded border, positioned near tapped term. Shows term name (colored with accent), definition text (with nested glossary term links), and related terms pills. Dismissible by clicking outside, pressing Escape, or scrolling. Component: src/components/ui/GlossaryPopover.tsx.
